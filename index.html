About Me

I turn raw footage and rough ideas into visuals people remember.

Bhopal, India

I'm Aalokit Harry — a self-taught 3D artist, video editor and graphic designer. Blender is my software of choice, though I stay tool-agnostic across the pipeline. My focus spans VFX, concept art, animation, 3D modeling and integration, alongside professional work as a graphic designer and video editor.

Whether it's a simulation built frame-by-frame in Blender, a brand identity, or a shoot cut for pace and rhythm, I care about the same things: craft, clarity and finishing what I start.
